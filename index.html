Frequently asked

How does Sunhaven estimate my burn time?

Estimates are based on WHO UV guidance, your skin type, and your sunscreen's SPF, with a built-in safety margin. Individual sensitivity varies — Sunhaven is guidance, not medical advice.

The app says it can't fetch the UV forecast.

UV data needs an internet connection and either location access or a chosen city. Check your connection, then tap Try again — or pick a city manually via Search your city (also available in Settings → Location).

I'm not receiving peak-UV or reapply alerts.

Make sure notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Sunhaven → Notifications. If they're off, the app shows a warning in Settings with a shortcut to fix it. Quiet hours (22:00–08:00) can also delay a peak alert.

Where is my data stored? Can I delete it?

Everything stays on your device — Sunhaven has no accounts and collects no data. Swipe left on any session in History to delete it, or delete the app to remove everything.

Which iPhones are supported?

Sunhaven requires iOS 17 or later. Live Activities appear on the lock screen and Dynamic Island; the Control Center button requires iOS 18.
